The code works once, for five minutes. After it is in, your phone shows six digits — check they match the ones that appear at the top of this page.

How this works, and what it can't do
  • Your phone does the sending. MeHub's server deletes a message as soon as a phone has it, and your chat keys never leave your phone, so your phone holds the only copy of your chats and passes them to this page itself. Keep it on and online.
  • The server still can't read anything. Your phone and this page work out a key between themselves that the server never sees, and everything between them is sealed with it.
  • Messages still go after 24 hours, here as on the phone. Nothing is saved on this computer: close the tab and it is gone.
  • Photos, files and voice messages show as a card naming the file. Open those on your phone.
